# LinkedIn Post Formats & Patterns

## Hook Patterns (pick the one that fits the data)

### 1. The Surprising Stat
```
[Specific number] just changed the way [audience] does [thing].
```
Example: "47% of Google searches now return an AI Overview. Zero clicks for nearly half."

### 2. The Bold Claim
```
[Common belief] is wrong. Here's what's actually happening.
```
Example: "SEO is dying? No. SEO is evolving faster than at any point in its 25-year history."

### 3. The Confession
```
I thought [X]. I was wrong. [What you learned instead].
```
Example: "I thought vibe coding was hype. Then I shipped a full product in 4 hours."

### 4. The Observation
```
Something shifted in [domain] and most people haven't noticed yet.
```

### 5. The Contrast
```
[What worked 2 years ago]: [X]
[What works now]: [Y]
The gap is wider than most people realize.
```

## CTA Templates (use the specific, not the generic)

**Bad CTA:** "What do you think?" / "Agree or disagree?"

**Good CTAs:**
- "Has your team started using [X]? What's the biggest friction point?"
- "Which of these three changes hit your workflow hardest?"
- "Are you building with AI or still watching from the sidelines?"
- "What's the one AI tool you'd never give up? Drop it below."
- "If you had to bet: will [X] happen by end of 2026? Yes or no in the comments."

## Storytelling Frameworks

### PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)
Best for: SEO+AI posts about disruption
```
[Problem: what's breaking]
[Agitate: why it's worse than people think]
[Solution: what the smart play is]
```

### Before/After/Bridge
Best for: Vibe Coding, AI in daily life
```
[Before: what the process looked like]
[After: what it looks like now with AI]
[Bridge: the one thing that made the difference]
```

### Observation → Insight → Implication
Best for: Thought leadership on AI trends
```
[Observation: factual, data-backed]
[Insight: what it actually means]
[Implication: what should change in how people act]
```

## Formatting Rules

- **Max line length**: 80 characters (mobile-first)
- **Paragraph size**: 1-3 lines max. Readers skim on mobile.
- **Emojis**: Use sparingly (0-2 per post). One in the hook if natural, one in takeaways.
- **Bold**: Not available in LinkedIn. Don't use markdown bold in the actual post.
- **ALL CAPS**: One word maximum, only for extreme emphasis. Avoid.
- **Hashtags**: 3-5, always at the very end, one space between each.

## What Kills LinkedIn Engagement

- Opening with "I" (algorithm buries it)
- Generic observations with no data
- Walls of text without white space
- Weak CTA ("let me know your thoughts")
- Too many hashtags (10+ looks like spam)
- Self-promotional tone
- Passive voice throughout
