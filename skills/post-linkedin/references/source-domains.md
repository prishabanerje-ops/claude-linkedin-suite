## Source Quality Signals

When evaluating sources, prefer:
- **Original research** over opinions (surveys, studies, proprietary data)
- **Specific numbers** over vague trends ("47% drop" beats "significant decline")
- **Recent dates** (2024-2026) over older content
- **Named experts** over anonymous analysis
- **Primary data** over secondary reporting where possible

Red flags to avoid:
- AI-generated content farms (thin, vague, no byline)
- PR pieces disguised as research
- Stats without methodology or sample size
- Sources older than 18 months for fast-moving topics like AI
